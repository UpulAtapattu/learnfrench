French Conjugation Master

A complete breakdown of the Present Tense. Select a module below to start training.

1. -ER Verbs Group 1

Regular -ER

Standard conjugation for the largest group of French verbs.

Ex: Parler, Aimer, Donner

Accent Changes

Verbs that change accents (e → è) to maintain sound.

Ex: Acheter, Préférer

-CER Verbs

Spelling change (ç) required in the Nous form.

Ex: Commencer, Lancer

-GER Verbs

Spelling change (ge) required in the Nous form.

Ex: Manger, Voyager

-ELER Verbs

Verbs that double the consonant (l → ll).

Ex: Appeler, Epeler
2. -IR Verbs Group 2

Type 1 (Regular)

The "ISS" pattern. Plural forms add -iss- before the ending.

Ex: Finir, Choisir, Réussir

Type 2 (Irregular Pattern)

No "-iss-". Endings attach directly to the stem.

Ex: Sortir, Dormir, Partir
3. -RE Verbs Group 3

Regular -RE

Standard pattern for regular verbs ending in -RE.

Ex: Vendre, Attendre, Perdre
4. Irregular Verbs Exceptions

Essential Irregulars

The top 25 most used verbs. These do not follow standard patterns and must be memorized.

Ex: Être, Avoir, Aller, Faire, Pouvoir
